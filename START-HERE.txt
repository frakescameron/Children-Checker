CHILDREN CHECK IN — ANDROID TABLET APP

1. Copy Children-Check-In.apk to your Android tablet (Android 8 or newer).
2. Tap the APK in the tablet's Files/Downloads app.
3. If Android asks, allow that app to install this APK, then tap Install.
4. Open Children Check In from the tablet's app list.

That's it. No host computer, server, account, Termux, or internet is needed.

CHECK IN A FAMILY
Open Check In. Select all the children under the same parent and tap Check In.
The next available beeper is assigned immediately to all selected children.
Give the parent the displayed number. There are no extra beeper/session fields
or parent confirmation screen.

CHECK OUT
The compact Hub shows each child with their beeper number. Use Check Out for
one child, or Group to check out everyone sharing that beeper. A shared beeper is
available again only after the last child in its group checks out.

HISTORY AND PRINTING
Tap Check-In History next to Manage Kids. Choose dates or tap All history.
You can search by child, parent, session label, or #beeper.
Print / Save PDF opens Android's print screen. Choose a configured printer
or Save as PDF. The app works offline; a Wi-Fi printer may need Wi-Fi.
Export CSV saves a spreadsheet-compatible copy of the filtered attendance.

YOUR EXISTING RECORDS
The APK includes the 7 saved kids and 15 check-in records in your uploaded ZIP.
They load once on the first launch. Existing active check-ins stay active.
Records from the old app have the session label "Imported records".
No later check-ins from the old server are automatically imported.

BACKUPS
Manage Kids > Save backup saves all kids and attendance to a JSON file.
Manage Kids > Restore backup restores that file after you confirm replacement.
Back up before uninstalling, clearing app data, or replacing the tablet.
Existing-Data-Backup.json is a separate copy of the original imported records.

Each tablet keeps its own records; tablets do not sync with one another.
